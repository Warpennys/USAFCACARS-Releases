# USAFCACARS Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** August 6, 2026  
**Last updated:** August 6, 2026  
**Application version at publication:** 1.0.4 Alpha

This Privacy Notice explains how **USA Flight Club** ("USAFC," "we," "us," or
"our") may collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information when you
use:

- The USAFCACARS Windows desktop application;
- The USAFCACARS phpVMS/API module;
- The USAFCACARS live map;
- USAFCACARS voice, radio, ATC, community, support, update, and download
  features;
- Official USAFCACARS release packages and documentation; and
- Related USA Flight Club websites and services that expressly link to this
  notice.

Official website:

[https://usaflightclub.net](https://usaflightclub.net)

Official USAFCACARS live map:

[https://usaflightclub.net/usafcacars/live](https://usaflightclub.net/usafcacars/live)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> USAFCACARS is proprietary software in active alpha development. Features,
> data practices, integrations, and technical architecture may change as
> development continues.
>
> The release notes for each build describe the features actually enabled in
> that build. Concept images and roadmap descriptions do not necessarily
> represent currently implemented data collection.

> [!NOTE]
> This notice is intended to describe USAFCACARS data practices in clear
> language. It should be reviewed before public release whenever a new
> analytics provider, communications provider, crash-reporting service,
> advertising component, account system, or other data-processing integration
> is added.

## 1. Scope

This notice applies to information processed through official USAFCACARS and
USA Flight Club systems that link to it.

It does not automatically apply to:

- Unofficial copies or modified versions of USAFCACARS;
- Third-party websites opened through Horizon Explorer;
- Microsoft Flight Simulator;
- Microsoft services;
- VATSIM, IVAO, Discord, GitHub, mapping, weather, chart, audio, or other
  third-party services;
- Third-party aircraft, add-ons, plug-ins, or utilities; or
- Websites and services that maintain their own privacy notices.

Third-party services process information according to their own terms and
privacy practices.

USA Flight Club administers the official USAFCACARS services and determines

The information collected depends on the build, enabled features, account

- Name;

- Callsign;
- Account identifier;
- Airline or division;
- Rank;
- Avatar or profile image;

- Profile preferences; and
- Account status information.

- Authentication tokens;
- Session identifiers;
- Token issue and expiration information;
- Login and logout times;
- Failed login attempts;
- Access roles;
- Authorization results; and
- Security events.

legal basis for processing personal information.

Where such laws apply, USAFC may rely on one or more of the following:

- **Performance of a contract or requested service:** to provide the
  USAFCACARS and USA Flight Club functions requested by the user;
- **Legitimate interests:** to operate, secure, troubleshoot, test, and
  improve USAFC systems, provided those interests are not overridden by the
  user's rights;
- **Consent:** where a feature requires optional access or consent, such as
  certain communications, recordings, integrations, or optional diagnostics;
- **Legal obligation:** where processing is necessary to comply with law; and
- **Protection of rights and safety:** where necessary to prevent abuse,
  protect users, investigate incidents, or establish legal claims.

Where processing depends on consent, the user may withdraw consent for
future processing, subject to technical and legal limitations.

## 10. Data Retention

USAFC retains information for only as long as reasonably necessary for the
purposes described in this notice, subject to operational, security, legal,
and technical needs.

Retention may vary by category.

### 10.1 Account and profile information

Account and profile information may be retained while an account remains
active and for a reasonable period afterward to administer records, resolve
disputes, protect services, or meet legal obligations.

### 10.2 Flight and PIREP records

Flight, PIREP, award, rank, and pilot-history records may be retained for a
long period because they form part of USA Flight Club's operational and
historical records.

Deletion of an account may not require deletion of every flight record where
retention is necessary for record integrity, audit history, dispute
resolution, or legal obligations.

### 10.3 Live telemetry

Current live-flight telemetry may be updated or replaced as new positions
arrive.

Historical telemetry, routes, trails, or event records may be retained when
needed for:

- PIREP processing;
- Flight review;
- Incident investigation;
- Statistics;
- testing;
- troubleshooting; or
- authorized historical features.

### 10.4 Voice information

Live voice audio is not intended to be retained by default.

Connection logs, room membership, technical diagnostics, and moderation
records may be retained for a reasonable period.

If recording is introduced, USAFC should publish the applicable retention
period and recording controls.

### 10.5 Support and diagnostic records

Support tickets, issue reports, attachments, and diagnostic logs may be
retained while a matter is active and afterward as reasonably necessary to:

- Confirm resolution;
- Identify repeated defects;
- Maintain security records;
- Improve the application; and
- Protect legal rights.

### 10.6 Backups

Information removed from active systems may remain temporarily in protected
backups until those backups are overwritten or expire through normal backup
rotation.

## 22. Alpha Development Commitments

During alpha development, USAFC should:

- Collect only information reasonably needed for an enabled feature;
- Avoid logging passwords and full authentication tokens;
- Mark diagnostic features clearly;
- Keep concept-only systems separate from active data collection;
- Review new integrations before enabling them;
- Document meaningful privacy changes in release notes;
- Restrict access to private test data;
- Avoid using real passenger or sensitive personal information in test data;
- Provide testers with a way to report privacy concerns; and
- Update this notice when practices materially change.
